About Me

My name is Aniket Bhosale, and I’m a passionate 3D enthusiast and VFX student. 3D is one of the biggest reasons I fell in love with cinema in the first place. It’s what made moviegoing feel magical to me. While most people were comfortable watching films at home, I was always the person excited to experience them on the biggest screen possible, just to feel that sense of depth, scale, and immersion that only 3D could offer.

Honestly, I could probably talk about 3D for hours and still not run out of things to say. The format has always fascinated me, and even today I still carry the same excitement and passion for it that I had growing up.

Ani3D is a space created for fellow 3D enthusiasts who still believe there’s something special about stereoscopic experiences. And if you’re reading this right now, chances are… you’re probably one of us too.

The exciting part is that creating 3D content is now more accessible than ever before.

Through this website, I want to help people discover how modern tools and AI-assisted workflows can make stereoscopic 3D creation possible even from home. From learning how to capture spatial and stereoscopic footage using newer iPhones, to experimenting with 2D-to-3D image and video conversion applications on PC like Owl3D, this platform is all about exploring the world of 3D creation in an approachable, creative, and passion-driven way.

One of the biggest reasons this website exists is not to promote the app itself, nor to simply become another 3D content library. For me, it’s about showing fellow 3D enthusiasts what’s truly possible with tools like these. If people enjoy the 3D catalogue and experiments created through Ani3D, then hopefully it encourages them to start exploring and creating their own stereoscopic 3D experiences too. And honestly, that would make me incredibly happy.

And finally, thank you for being a part of Ani3D. I genuinely hope you enjoy the 3D videos, experiments, and experiences shared throughout this journey as much as I enjoy creating them.

Experience cinema differently.

Experience everything in 3D.
