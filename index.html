Experience Cinema Differently

Ani 3D is home to The 3D Experience Project, a passion-driven initiative dedicated to exploring visual media through stereoscopic 3D.

Using AI-assisted 2D-to-3D image and video conversion with Owl3D, alongside original projects shot in stereoscopic 3D using newer iPhones, the goal is to explore as many forms of visual entertainment as possible through the lens of depth and immersive presentation. From Hollywood blockbusters and regional cinema to television, anime, animated features, horror films, action spectacles, internet culture, memes, reels, YouTube Shorts, website browsing in 3D, motion graphics, gameplay clips ranging from mobile gaming to PC gaming, colour and black-and-white content, UI experiments, and random online visuals, this project exists to push the boundaries of what can be experienced in 3D.

The content spans 20+ genres, multiple film industries, and languages from around the world, creating a global 3D showcase driven entirely by passion and curiosity.

About Us

ANI3D

Ani3D is more than just a library of short-form 3D content. It is a passion-driven initiative dedicated to exploring what visual media could feel like through stereoscopic 3D experiences.

I'm Aniket Bhosale, a passionate 3D enthusiast exploring what cinema, television, internet culture, and everyday visual media could feel like in stereoscopic 3D. Ever since I fell in love with the format, I've always been fascinated by one simple question:

"What would this look like in 3D?"

That curiosity became the foundation of this project.

Some of these films were originally released in 3D.

Some never received a 3D version at all.

And some simply had incredible untapped 3D potential.

This project also explores iconic scenes reimagined in 3D, alongside native vs AI-converted comparisons to better understand how stereoscopic depth can transform the viewing experience.

Designed as a one-year journey, this initiative is about preserving the excitement and culture surrounding 3D while also experimenting with content audiences never had the chance to experience stereoscopically. It's a creative playground built around one idea:

"What if?"

But more than anything, Ani3D is about community.

It's about connecting with like-minded people who still appreciate the magic of immersive visuals, theatrical depth, and the unique feeling that 3D can create. Whether you're a lifelong stereoscopic fan, someone curious about the format, or even an aspiring creator interested in 3D filmmaking and conversion workflows, this space is meant to be shared with people who carry that same excitement for the medium.

Hopefully, by the end of this journey, both me and the community built around Ani3D will have experienced almost everything possible in 3D. That's the experience I want this project to become: a shared journey driven by curiosity, experimentation, and a passion for immersive storytelling.

And honestly, I'd be more than happy to have more and more people join me along the way.

"Experience cinema differently. Experience everything in 3D."

Owl3D Technology

Some time back, I came across an application called Owl3D, a PC-based tool that allows users to convert 2D images and videos into stereoscopic 3D using AI-assisted depth conversion. As someone who has always been passionate about 3D, I was genuinely surprised by how impressive and immersive some of the results could look.

What I personally enjoy most about the application is the amount of creative control it gives users. From different 3D formats and depth strengths to multiple rendering styles and conversion approaches, the software allows creators to experiment with a wide range of possibilities depending on the type of experience they want to create.

And no, this isn't a sponsored promotion or a paid collaboration.

I'm simply talking about a tool that genuinely helped me explore stereoscopic 3D creation in a way that previously felt inaccessible outside professional industry workflows. Even the free version offers a surprisingly large number of features, which is exactly what I currently use myself for many of the projects and experiments uploaded on this website.

As for how good the 3D output is, I'll leave that for you to decide. Every piece of 3D content converted using Owl3D and featured on this website is available for everyone to experience, enjoy, and judge for themselves.

So why am I so excited about it?

Because tools like Owl3D make stereoscopic 3D experimentation more accessible than ever before, allowing enthusiasts and creators to explore ideas that previously required far more specialized equipment and workflows.

Even though 3D may not be as mainstream as it once was, the passion for the format still exists among enthusiasts around the world. This project exists to help keep that excitement alive while building a community of people who still love the magic of immersive visual storytelling and appreciate the tools that make those creative possibilities more accessible than ever.
